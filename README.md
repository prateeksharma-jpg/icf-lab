# ICF Learning Lab

Interactive companion to *Introduction to Corporate Finance* (IIM Udaipur, 2026), Sessions 11–20.

**Session 11: Cost of capital and hurdle rates (Marriott case)**
- **Start here:** the balance-sheet blender, firm vs division vs project hurdle rates, the 7-step recipe and a formula sheet.
- **Hurdle rates:** why one firm-wide WACC misprices divisions, with an interactive chart and a capital-committee game.
- **WACC Machine:** simulate leverage, with the cost of debt following a synthetic rating; decomposes K<sub>e</sub> and WACC.
- **Marriott lab:** rebuilds the case solution live from the exhibits; change any assumption and see what moves.
- **Real companies:** today's Marriott, Hilton, McDonald's, Aramark and nine Indian companies (data as of Nov 2026).
- **Concept checks:** 14 reasoning questions with explanations.
- **Concept application practice:** 5 real-world cases (2 medium, then 3 hard) that together use every step of the session, with hints and step-by-step solutions that unlock after an attempt.

**Session 12: Valuing equity with dividends (DDM, growth, PVGO)**
- **Start here:** book value vs value (Amazon 2005, Nestlé India, ONGC), the three DDM inputs, and full session notes.
- **DDM Builder:** one-period, stable-growth, two-stage and three-stage models, with every dividend and its PV, the terminal value, and a sensitivity table. The class examples are one click away.
- **Growth engine & PVGO:** retention × ROE year by year (book value, EPS, DPS), payout changes, and value = assets in place + PVGO.
- **How high can growth go?:** the Apple vs World point, that stable growth must stay below the economy's growth (g < R<sub>f</sub>), and why value explodes as g → r.
- **Real companies:** implied growth, PVGO and retention × ROE for nine Indian dividend payers (data as of 25 Nov 2026), plus a dividend-model fit check (Infosys vs Tata Steel).
- **Concept checks:** 14 reasoning questions with explanations.
- **Concept application practice:** 5 cases (2 medium, then 3 hard).

Open `index.html` in a browser; there is no build step. Links: `#s12/builder` opens a session and tab; old links without a session prefix (such as `#cases`) open Session 11. Progress is stored only in the student's browser.

Market data are approximate teaching inputs compiled from public sources (screener.in, stockanalysis.com, Yahoo Finance price and dividend histories, company filings, Damodaran's datasets). They are not investment advice.
